E2E Process Optimization

Redesigning operational workflows to improve efficiency and reduce errors.

A promotional process across multiple teams was generating invisible friction — duplicated work, siloed decision-making, and fragmented data. I mapped the complete flow, identified friction points, and redesigned the operation around a shared model, incorporating data automation where it had the greatest impact. The result was less rework and greater operational clarity across teams.

  • Challenge: Understand a complex promotional process with multiple areas and hidden friction points.
  • Decision: Create a shared model that aligned timelines, roles and responsibilities across teams.
  • Outcome: Less rework and greater agility, with unified templates, automated tasks, and clearer operational ownership.

Medical Platform / Webinars

Optimizing content consumption for healthcare professionals.

Doctors needed to access critical educational content from any device, but striking a balance between clarity, information volume, and ease of navigation across three screen sizes was the real challenge. I defined the navigation, grid layout, and reusable components to maintain consistency without having to rebuild everything from scratch. The result was a clearer, cleaner, and more scalable interface across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

  • Challenge: Design a cross-device experience that maintained clarity and consistency in a regulated industry.
  • Decision: Build a UI system with reusable components and a clear visual structure to present information effectively.
  • Outcome: A responsive, consistent and scalable experience across mobile, tablet and desktop.

Vehicle Inspection App

Field UI optimization focused on readability and operational performance.

A vehicle inspection app used by inspectors across different age groups had legibility issues that made data capture difficult in the field. The challenge wasn't just visual — it was about building trust in the data. I redesigned the visual system with optimized typography and contrast, and restructured the flows to make each step clearer and easier to follow.

  • Challenge: Improve readability and capture clarity for diverse profiles in demanding field conditions.
  • Decision: Optimize typography, contrast and flows to make information clearer and more reliable during inspections.
  • Outcome: A more consistent visual system and a capture experience adapted to different conditions and user profiles.

Financial Advisory System

Redesign to centralize tasks, goals and client tracking for advisors.

Advisors worked on a dense intranet where multiple dashboards made it hard to find key insights. Through heuristic evaluations and tests with expert users, I simplified the visual structure, removed noise and prioritized the information most relevant to their daily work. The result was a clearer interface and a more consistent structure for the platform.

  • Challenge: Simplify dense panels with multiple charts and indicators without losing data depth.
  • Decision: Create a modular framework and UX guidelines to structure and standardize the platform.
  • Outcome: A clearer, more consistent interface that made it easier to interpret key information.
